### Features

- A thing that you ignored can come back. Before, ✖ **Ignore** acted at once and left the message without a button, so a press that slipped beside ⏰ **Later** hid an issue and every later comment of its author for 30 days, or muted a workflow that fails on `main`, and the only repair was to remember it and look at GitHub. Now an ignored thing offers ↩️ **Bring back**, which opens it again, in place, as it was when you ignored it: its buttons come back, its topic loses ✅, and it no longer expires. A thing that waited for a draft, a fix or a rerun comes back with the button that starts one again. What happened on GitHub while it was ignored, such as a comment, shows with its next event. The button comes with what you ignore from this release on: the message of a thing ignored before keeps no button. It needs no new permission and no new secret.
- Write `/open` to the bot, and it lists what still waits for you, in one message: each thing that is open and still offers a button, grouped by repository, linked to its page on GitHub, with when ⏰ **Later** brings it back. Done and ignored things stay out, and with nothing open it says so in one line. Before, the topics of the chat were the only overview, where done, ignored and open things stand side by side. It only reads, acts on nothing and sends nothing to the control repository; `/help` mentions it, and the set-up guide shows how to register `/open`, `/status` and `/help` in the menu of Telegram. It needs no new permission and no new secret.
