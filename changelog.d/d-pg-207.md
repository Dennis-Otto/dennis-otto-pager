### Bug fixes

- The album of a visual change no longer shows an animation as three pictures that don't fit together. Before, it showed the changed pixels of one frame, such as step 4, beside the first frame of the animation before and after, so the change looked like another picture. Now each changed picture comes as one photo, the place of its change before and after of the same frame with the changes framed in red, captioned with how much it changed and, for an animation, the frame, such as *frame 4 of 7*; this needs the visual check of the next release of the blueprint. With an older visual check, an animation shows its changed pixels alone, captioned as one frame of the animation. With one photo per picture, an album now holds up to ten changed pictures.
